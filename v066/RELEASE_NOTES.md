# Chess Doctor v0.6.6 Beta

## Guided visual lessons

The v0.6.5 visual story was understandable but too fast and too far from its trigger on mobile.

v0.6.6 changes the flow:
- auto-scroll to the board when visual explanation starts;
- visual lesson controls directly above the board;
- step counter and plain-language caption;
- Back / Next navigation;
- optional autoplay;
- slower ~1s piece motion and longer holds.

The chess-analysis and causal-trust rules are unchanged.
