# Grandmaster game data

25 historical games, curated and accessed on 2026-10-11. Each entry in
`grandmaster-games.json` carries its source and corroborating URL, access date,
terms note, replay length, and SHA-256 of the complete canonical UCI sequence.
The source records were compared for player identity, year, result and every
move, after replay through the bundled chess.js. Some collections may share an
upstream record; this is corroboration, not a claim of independent historical
scholarship. Rejected candidates with differing scores/results were excluded.

Only factual players, event, dates, result and moves are bundled. All third-party
comments, annotations, diagrams, NAGs, evaluation tags and side variations were
removed. Unclear annotation licenses are not treated as permission to copy text.
No external book prose or database descriptions are included. Russian names
are transliterations; theme tags were assigned for this collection. All lesson
explanations are generated by this application's rules from verified structure.

Sources: usefulmove/chessdb (factual records from annotated collections),
ValdemarOrn/Chess (factual scores only), brianerdelyi/ChessPGN (declared CC0),
ANTLR PGN fixtures, and michael1241/wcc_analysis (mirror of Lichess championship
studies, factual records only). Exact URLs and per-source terms notes are in the
machine-readable manifest. Licenses for chess.js and Stockfish remain in vendor/.

Validation: `node tests/library.cjs`. The manifest checksum covers all moves,
including promotions, rather than notation formatting. Source comparison was a
release-time research step; CI performs an offline replay and checksum check.
