# Chess Doctor v0.6.8 Beta

## More readable chess motion

Inspired by the supplied animation reference, Chess Doctor now shows the path of a move instead of relying on motion alone.

Before movement:
- source and target are highlighted;
- direction arrow appears;
- capture target is visibly warned.

During movement:
- animation is slower and easier to track;
- captured piece fades near impact.

After movement:
- final position is held;
- direction arrow remains visible for orientation.

Applied to both Doctor guided lessons and Master Lesson move demos.
